All Features

Packed with 10 advanced features for the ultimate recoil control experience

PID Controller — 90% Stability

Advanced Proportional-Integral-Derivative controller provides exceptional 90% stability during sustained fire. The system continuously monitors and adjusts compensation in real-time for pixel-perfect accuracy.

Breathing System — Natural Aiming

Simulates natural breathing patterns for long-range scope engagement. Creates organic mouse movements that mimic real player behavior, making your aim undetectable.

Adaptive Recoil — Smart Learning

Our AI learns your unique firing patterns and automatically adjusts compensation over time. The more you play, the smarter and more accurate the system becomes.

Burst Mode — 2-10 Shot Control

Configure precise burst control from 2 to 10 consecutive shots with automatic stop. Perfect for weapons that require controlled tap-firing for maximum accuracy.

Micro-Adjustment — Pro Precision

Fine-tune compensation down to the pixel level. Professional players can make micro-adjustments for each weapon type and attachment combination.

Auto Detection — Tab/Grenade

Automatically pauses when opening inventory (Tab) or switching to grenades (keys 4+5+6). No more accidental activation during non-combat situations.

Emergency Stop — G Key (5s)

Press G key for instant 5-second emergency stop. Perfect for when you need to quickly disengage without accessing the full settings menu.

Bilingual — AR/EN Support

Full Arabic and English interface support. Switch languages anytime with all menus, tooltips, and notifications translated for your convenience.

Visualizer — Recoil Pattern

Interactive visualizer shows real-time recoil patterns being applied. See exactly how the system compensates for each weapon's unique recoil behavior.

Save System — Multiple Presets

Save unlimited presets for different weapons, attachments, and scenarios. Quick-switch between configurations without manual reconfiguration.
